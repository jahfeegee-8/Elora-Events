Who We Are

Born from a Love
of Beautiful Moments

Elora Events was founded with a single conviction: that every celebration — no matter the scale — deserves to be executed with intention, creativity, and heart. Based in Kempton Park, Johannesburg, we serve clients across the entire Gauteng region, bringing a boutique studio sensibility to events of every kind.

What sets us apart is our integrated approach. Rather than outsourcing your planning, decoration, and catering to separate vendors who may never speak to each other, Elora Events handles it all in house. Your vision moves seamlessly from a first conversation into a cohesive, beautifully executed event — with one team, one point of contact, and zero gaps.

From intimate family gatherings and boho-chic picnics to grand marquee receptions and polished corporate functions, our portfolio spans the full spectrum of life's big moments. We bring the same care and creativity to a birthday picnic for twenty as we do to a wedding reception for two hundred.
