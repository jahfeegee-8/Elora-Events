Event Planning

Stress-Free Planning, From First Idea to Final Bow

Planning an event involves hundreds of decisions, dozens of vendors, and endless logistics. Elora Events takes that weight off your shoulders entirely. We manage the full planning process — so on your event day, the only thing you need to do is arrive and enjoy.

We serve weddings, corporate functions, milestone birthdays, baby showers, and private celebrations across Johannesburg and greater Gauteng.

  • Full event concept development and mood boarding
  • Venue scouting, selection, and liaison
  • Vendor sourcing and management (photographers, DJ, florist, etc.)
  • Budget planning and ongoing cost tracking
  • Timeline and run-of-show creation
  • Guest list and RSVP management
  • Day-of coordination and on-site management
  • Post-event wrap-up and vendor payments

Event Decoration

Turning Spaces into Extraordinary Experiences

Decoration is where your event's personality comes to life. Our creative team designs and installs every visual element — from towering floral installations to hand-folded napkins — transforming any venue into a space that feels uniquely yours.

We work across all styles: boho-chic, classic elegance, garden romance, contemporary glamour, and everything in between. No theme is too bold or too refined.

  • Personalised theme development and style consultation
  • Floral arrangements — centrepieces, arches, bouquets, and garlands
  • Table styling — linens, charger plates, napkins, place settings
  • Balloon installations, garlands, and bespoke balloon arches
  • Marquee and tent draping, ceiling treatments, and canopy décor
  • Ambient and feature lighting design
  • Signage — welcome boards, seating charts, table numbers
  • Full installation, styling, and post-event breakdown

Catering

Menus That Are as Memorable as the Moment

Great food is the centrepiece of every great celebration. Our catering service brings professional culinary quality to events of all sizes — from elegant cocktail parties to seated banquet dinners — with menus crafted around your tastes, dietary needs, and event style.

Every menu is tailored collaboratively. We handle all the food, equipment, and staffing so you and your guests can simply savour every bite.

  • Personalised menu design and tasting consultation
  • Canapés, finger foods, and cocktail station menus
  • Buffet-style and family-share table menus
  • Plated fine-dining courses for formal functions
  • Grazing tables and charcuterie boards
  • Dietary accommodation — vegetarian, vegan, halal, and gluten-free options
  • Professional service staff and waitrons
  • Crockery, glassware, and serving equipment supply
  • Full setup, service, and post-event cleanup
