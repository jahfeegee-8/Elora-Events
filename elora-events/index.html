Our Story

Gauteng's Boutique Events Studio

Based in Kempton Park, Johannesburg, Elora Events was built on one simple belief: your special moments deserve extraordinary care. We are a full-service events company offering planning, decoration, and catering under one roof — removing the stress of managing multiple vendors and giving you back the joy of your occasion.

Whether you're planning a wedding, a milestone birthday, a baby shower, or a corporate gathering across Gauteng, our team brings creativity, precision, and warmth to every single detail.
